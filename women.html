THE STACKLY EDIT

Know the fabric. Love the fit.

01

Care with intention

Wash cotton gently, let linen air dry, and fold knitwear. Always follow the care label.

02

Measure before choosing

Compare chest or bust, waist, and hip measurements. Leave room for your preferred silhouette.

03

Choose your silhouette

A regular fit sits neatly, while relaxed shapes add room through the body. Compare the intended fit with a favourite piece you already own.
