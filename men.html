THE STACKLY EDIT

Outfits for changing days.

Combine a linen shirt with straight trousers, then add a textured knit when the evening cools. Keep colours close for effortless combinations.

A useful wardrobe reflects the days you actually dress for. Begin with dependable pieces, then add textures and silhouettes that bring variety to familiar combinations.

Style it your way

Comfort depends on more than a size label. Consider how the garment moves when you sit, walk, and reach, and compare the fit notes with a piece you already enjoy wearing.

THE STACKLY EDIT

Know the fabric. Love the fit.

01

Care with intention

Wash cotton gently, let linen air dry, and fold knitwear. Always follow the care label.

02

Measure before choosing

Compare chest or bust, waist, and hip measurements. Leave room for your preferred silhouette.

03

Choose your silhouette

A regular fit sits neatly, while relaxed shapes add room through the body. Compare the intended fit with a favourite piece you already own.
