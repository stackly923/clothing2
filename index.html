THE STACKLY EDIT

It starts with how it feels.

01

Cotton

Soft against the skin and easy to care for. A dependable foundation for everyday dressing.

02

Linen

A natural, textured fabric with an airy feel. Embrace its gentle creases as part of its character.

03

Denim

Structure that softens with wear. Choose the rise and leg shape that work for your movement.

04

Knit

Comfortable texture for layering. Fold knitwear between wears to help keep its shape.

05

Viscose

A fluid fabric with a soft drape, suited to easy dresses and occasion silhouettes. Handle gently and follow the garment label.

06

Jersey

A knitted construction with natural movement. Comfortable for tees and relaxed layers; reshape gently after washing.
