THE STACKLY EDIT

Measure with confidence.

01

Chest & bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest point, keeping it level and comfortably loose.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waist, without holding your breath or pulling the tape tight.

03

Hip

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.

04

Inseam

Measure from the top of the inside leg to the preferred hem; a well-fitting pair can be a useful reference.

THE STACKLY EDIT

Make room for your preference.

01

Regular fit

A balanced shape with comfortable room through the body.

02

Relaxed fit

Extra ease for movement and a softer silhouette.

03

Tailored fit

A closer shape; check the shoulders and seated waist carefully.

04

Between sizes

Choose according to the closest fitting area and your preference. Ask for garment measurements when possible.
